# SPATIAL validation contract v2

Mandatory: CRS/projection and geometry audit; residual dependence before/after; spatial block, buffered, or leave-region-out validation aligned with deployment; non-spatial same-basis baseline; scale/neighborhood sensitivity; boundary/extrapolation tests; uncertainty coverage where predictive. Random K-fold cannot pass when spatial dependence creates leakage. Projection/geometry failure is FAIL or REVISE before metric interpretation. No residual spatial signal supports simplification rather than forcing a spatial model.

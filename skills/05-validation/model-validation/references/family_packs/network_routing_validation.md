# NETWORK_ROUTING validation contract v2

Preregister and bind: conservation/capacity/visit/depot/time-window/subtour checks; recomputed objective; exact certificate or justified lower bound/gap; baseline under identical constraints; perturbation of costs/demand/capacity; disconnected/infeasible edge cases; runtime and seed stability where approximate. A high-quality route with any hard violation is FAIL. Missing bound/gap when an exact route was feasible, or unexplained approximate escalation, is REVISE. Static validation cannot support claims under material unmodeled congestion.

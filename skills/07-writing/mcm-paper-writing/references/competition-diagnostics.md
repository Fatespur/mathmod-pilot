# Competition narrative diagnostics

Apply to main prose, including abstract and conclusion; exclude references, appendix, code, formulas and quoted review instructions from phrase counts. Inspect semantics, not keywords alone. A necessary mathematical negation or a concise positive scope is not automatically defensive writing. Attach section/line, excerpt, evidence source, severity and proposed action to each finding.

Monitor: 不能证明、不能说明、不能据此、不意味着、不代表、不构成、不能排除、无法排除、不能宣称、尚未建立、尚未证明、并不等价于 (including semantic variants).

`DEFENSIVE_WRITING`: the same limitation mentioned at least twice → WARNING candidate. Two complete disclosures of the same limitation → `OVER_DEFENSIVE_WRITING = HIGH`. Also HIGH: at least two consecutive defensive sentences after a result; a paragraph's negative-scope text longer than its positive result explanation; more than one defensive/scope sentence or extensive limitation discussion in abstract; conclusion repeating a systemic limitation; pervasive scientific-audit jargon in body. Count only semantically confirmed spans, not every sentence containing “不能”.

Repair: retain necessary boundaries and adverse evidence, remove duplicate explanations, use positive scope, move systemic detail to model evaluation. Do not ask for a mandatory limitation after each result. Repeated short scope phrases needed for standalone interpretation are not full repeated disclosures.

`UNDERSELLING = HIGH`: evidenced advantage left unsummarized; strong result buried; stability evidence without its meaning; demonstrated method contribution without a supported simple-method contrast; abstract omits strengths; useful figure evidence unmentioned. Compare actual evidence to exposure, not to an invented ideal. A manuscript already highlighting several strengths may receive PARTIAL rather than blanket YES.

`FIGURE_NARRATIVE_INSUFFICIENT = HIGH`: a complex multi-stage paper lacks an overview/equivalent visual, or a key process/result lacks adequate visual support. Review framework, data, core result, comparison and relevant robustness coverage with per-role applicability. Route framework work to cumcm-academic-flowchart and quantitative figures to scipilot-figure-cumcm; never fail by absolute count.

Metrics definitions (ratios 0–1): result_first_coverage = reviewed core question/result units with answer before extended methods/disclaimers divided by reviewed units; core_answer_visibility = task answers quickly locatable / task answers; strength_visibility = supported selected strengths adequately exposed / selected strengths. No denominator → null. framework_figure_present records actual overview/equivalent presence; core_figure_count counts figures assigned a core evidence role. defensive_phrase_count counts monitored phrase occurrences after scope exclusions; repeated_limitation_clusters counts semantic limitation groups with repeated full disclosures; abstract_limitation_sentences counts semantic qualifying/defensive sentences; over_defensive_paragraphs counts confirmed offending paragraphs; underselling_findings counts confirmed findings. Record denominators, scope exclusions and locations in diagnostic_evidence. Automated counts are candidates until semantic review; no script may claim scientific evidence is strong from keyword frequency.
